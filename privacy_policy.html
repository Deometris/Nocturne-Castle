Nocturne Castle - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Nocturne Castle (the "Game") handles. The Game is made by David Brien ("we", "us").

The short version

The Game does not collect, send or sell your personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app tracking. Everything the Game saves stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

To work, the Game saves the following files in its own storage area on your device:

  • Game progress: your current run (cards, items, gold, map position) so you can continue later.
  • Settings: options such as volume and display choices.
  • Achievements and run logs: records of what you have done in the Game, kept as plain files so you can look at them.

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it. It is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and the Game does not request those permissions.

Third-party services

  • Android / Google Play: if you install the Game from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under its own privacy policy. The Game itself adds no Google services such as ads or analytics.
  • Steam (PC version only): if you play the PC version on Steam, Steam can record achievements and leaderboard scores for your Steam account under Valve's privacy policy. The phone version does not use Steam.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and contains fantasy violence. Because it collects no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the Game ever changes how it handles information (for example, by adding online features), we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
